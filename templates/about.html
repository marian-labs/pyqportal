About the Portal

Meet the team behind PYQ Portal and learn about our mission

Marian College Kuttikkanam

Built by students,
for students.

A free, fast, and always-available archive of previous year question papers — designed to make every student's exam preparation a little easier.

01 — Our Mission

We believe every student deserves access to quality study resources — without barriers, without cost.

The PYQ Portal was born from a simple frustration: The problem was simple but real. Question papers were mostly available only in the physical library and not in digital form. Students often had to depend on seniors to share copies, making access difficult — especially during exam time.
