Before You Upload
Remove Student Names

Before scanning or uploading, ensure you cross out, cover, or blur any names or roll numbers written on the question paper to protect student privacy.

Scan and Upload

Use our built-in "Scan Paper Pages via Camera" button below to snap, crop, and compile pages into a clean PDF, or upload a pre-scanned PDF directly.

Keep Files Under 5 MB

Maximum upload size is 5 MB. If your scanned PDF is larger, compress it using free tools like ILovePDF or Smallpdf before submitting.
